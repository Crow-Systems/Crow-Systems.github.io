# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

Crow Systems respects user privacy and is committed to protecting personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

* Name
* Company information
* Email address
* Phone number
* Consultation details
* Audio submissions
* Technical browser information
* Website usage analytics

---

## 3. How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to:

* Respond to inquiries
* Provide consulting services
* Improve website functionality
* Process submissions
* Send requested communications
* Maintain security and reliability

Crow Systems does not sell personal information.

---

## 4. Audio Submission Handling

Audio recordings submitted through the website may be temporarily stored and processed for consultation review purposes.

Users should avoid submitting highly sensitive, confidential, or regulated information unless a formal agreement is in place.

---

## 5. Cookies & Analytics

The website may use:

* Essential cookies
* Performance analytics
* Anonymous usage tracking

Analytics data is used to improve user experience and website performance.

---

## 6. Third-Party Providers

Crow Systems may use third-party providers for:

* Hosting
* File storage
* Form processing
* Analytics
* Email delivery

These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies.

---

## 7. Data Security

Crow Systems implements reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect submitted information.

However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

---

## 8. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for:

* Business operations
* Legal obligations
* Service delivery
* Security monitoring

Unused or outdated information may be periodically deleted.

---

## 9. User Rights

Users may request:

* Access to their personal information
* Correction of inaccurate data
* Deletion requests where applicable
* Communication preferences updates

Requests may be submitted to:

[privacy@crowsys.chrislabs.net](mailto:privacy@crowsys.chrislabs.net)

---

## 10. International Users

Users accessing the website from outside the operating region acknowledge that information may be processed in jurisdictions different from their own.

---

## 11. Children's Privacy

The website and services are not intended for children under 13 years of age.

Crow Systems does not knowingly collect information from children.

---

## 12. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

---

## 13. Contact Information

Questions regarding privacy may be directed to:

[privacy@crowsys.chrislabs.net](mailto:privacy@crowsys.chrislabs.net)
